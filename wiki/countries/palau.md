# Palau 🇵🇼 | パラオ — Article 6.2 Host Country

=== "🇬🇧 English"

    > Last updated: **2026-08-02**

    ## Overview

    Palau is a Japan JCM partner (2014) — a Pacific small island state with negligible GHG emissions but extraordinary marine biodiversity and a **protected marine area covering over 80% of its EEZ**. JCM projects are small-scale (island solar, energy efficiency). Blue carbon (seagrass, mangroves) is the primary NBS narrative, though blue carbon methodologies are still maturing. Palau's primary climate role is as a global advocate for ambition rather than a credit supplier.

    ## ITMO Transfer

    Palau completed its first-ever JCM ITMO transfer to Japan on **June 2, 2026** (JC decision May 16, 2026) — four small-scale island solar projects, vintage 2021, all acquired by MOEJ:

    | Project | Volume |
    |---------|-------:|
    | PW001 | 32 tCO₂e |
    | PW002 | 12 tCO₂e |
    | PW003 | 34 tCO₂e |
    | PW004 | 116 tCO₂e |
    | **Total** | **194 tCO₂e** |

    This makes Palau the first Pacific SIDS to complete a JCM ITMO transfer, and Japan's 3rd JCM transfer overall (after Thailand and Maldives, before Mongolia). See [ITMO Tracker](../itmo_tracker.md) for the full chronological list.

    ## ITMO移転

    パラオは**2026年6月2日**（JC決定は5月16日）に日本への初のJCM ITMO移転を完了した。島嶼小規模太陽光4案件、2021ビンテージ、すべて環境省が取得：

    | プロジェクト | 量 |
    |------------|---:|
    | PW001 | 32 tCO₂e |
    | PW002 | 12 tCO₂e |
    | PW003 | 34 tCO₂e |
    | PW004 | 116 tCO₂e |
    | **合計** | **194 tCO₂e** |

    これにより、パラオは太平洋島嶼国（SIDS）として初めてJCM ITMO移転を完了した国となり、日本にとってはタイ・モルディブに次ぐ3件目のJCM移転となった（モンゴルより前）。時系列の全リストは[ITMOトラッカー](../itmo_tracker.md)を参照。
